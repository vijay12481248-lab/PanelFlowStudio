scene: s4 | stage | hopeful | Reframing | I am excited.
beat: medium | diagram | priya | Here is a clever trick: tell yourself I am excited, instead of I am calm. Excitement and anxiety feel similar in the body. | Priya with a bright smile pointing at two flat yellow speech bubbles, one with a happy face and one with a worried face joined by an arrow.
beat: medium | diagram | priya | Studies suggest people who reframe nerves as excitement often perform better than people who try to force themselves to calm down. | Priya giving a thumbs up beside a flat yellow rising arrow and a small star.

beat: medium | diagram | priya | A little nervous energy can actually help. Moderate arousal tends to sharpen focus. Too much hurts, and too little makes you sleepy. | Priya holding a flat yellow upside-down U shaped curve on the navy backdrop with a small star at the top, smiling.
scene: s5 | living_room | funny | Breathing and practice | Calming tools.
beat: medium | diagram | priya | Slow breathing helps too. Breathe in for four, out for six. A longer out-breath tells your body it can settle. | Priya breathing out slowly with a calm smile, a soft blue glow around her.
beat: close | stage | maya | Maya breathes. In, two, three, four. Out, two, three, four, five, six. Her shoulders drop. | Maya with her eyes closed taking a slow breath and her shoulders lowering, a calm face.
beat: medium | diagram | priya | Practice helps most. Practice out loud, in front of a mirror, a friend, or your cat. Familiar feels safer. | Priya practicing a speech in front of a flat yellow mirror shape with an animated smile.
