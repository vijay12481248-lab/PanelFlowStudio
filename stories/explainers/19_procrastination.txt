beat: medium | diagram | priya | So how do you break it? First, shrink the first step. Not write the report. Just open the file and write one ugly sentence. | Priya holding up one finger with a bright smile, a tiny flat yellow document shape beside her.
beat: close | living_room | maya | Maya sets a two-minute timer. Her only job is to write one terrible sentence. She writes three. | Maya typing at her laptop with a pleased small smile and relaxed happy eyes, a small yellow timer glowing beside her.
beat: medium | diagram | priya | Starting is the hardest part. Once you begin, the feeling of dread often fades, and the task becomes smaller than it looked. | Priya standing beside a big flat yellow mountain shape that gets smaller to a little hill with a hopeful smile.
beat: medium | diagram | priya | Second, make an if-then plan. If it is nine, then I open the report. A plan decides in advance. | Priya holding two flat yellow cards, one with a clock and one with a document, joined by an arrow, with a confident smile.
beat: wide | diagram | | Third, reduce friction. Put your phone in another room. Close the tabs. Objects only. | a phone in a box, an open laptop
beat: medium | diagram | priya | Fourth, name the feeling. I feel anxious about doing this badly. Naming it can make it less scary. | Priya with a hand on her chest and a gentle smile, a small flat yellow speech bubble beside her.
