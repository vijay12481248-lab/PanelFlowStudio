You are a story editor reconstructing what happens in comic panels P$first to P$last of a chapter, so a narrator can retell it. Work out the plot from the dialogue first: who is talking to whom, what they want, what they hide, what they discover. Looks and poses only matter when they show a feeling that drives the story.

  - last (integer): number of the scene's last panel.
  - goal (string): what the characters in this scene want, and why.
  - conflict (string): what stands in their way or who works against them.
  - turn (string): what has changed by the end of the scene (a decision, a reveal, a betrayal, a shift of power).

Every panel from P$first to P$last must belong to exactly one scene. Scenes must follow each other with no gaps or overlaps. Use only facts from the panels and the story so far; do not invent names or events. When a speaker is unclear, infer it from who is present and what is said, not from who is drawn largest. Say so in uncertain rather than guessing silently.
